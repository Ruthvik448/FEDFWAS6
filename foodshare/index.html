Tips to Reduce Food Waste

  • ✅ Plan your meals
  • ✅ Store food properly
  • ✅ Share surplus food
  • ✅ Donate instead of discarding
  • ✅ Use a FIFO method for pantry items
  • ✅ Compost unavoidable food scraps
